Call the 24/7 Line.

To reach Palisades Park 24/7 Plumbing, call the 24/7 line. It is open every hour of every day, including nights, weekends, and holidays. This site has no contact form, because a form cannot ask you the follow-up question that matters in the first minute of a leak.

Below is what to have ready, what we will ask, and what to do while you wait. If your problem is a plain question about a project, the same line works.

What to say on the call.

A short, clear description gets the right plumber on the way faster.

  1. 1

    Say where you are.

    Give the street, the town, and the ZIP. We serve Palisades Park 07650 and the nearby towns listed on our service areas page.

  2. 2

    Describe what is happening.

    Is water coming from a pipe, a drain, a ceiling, or a heater? Is it still running?

  3. 3

    Say what you have done.

    Tell us whether you have shut the main valve, turned off the breaker to a wet area, or stopped using drains.

  4. 4

    Mention access.

    A basement, a locked building, a landlord, or a shared line changes how a plumber plans the visit.

  5. 5

    Ask about the price.

    A licensed plumber should give you a clear price before work starts. If you are unsure, ask them to explain it.

While you wait for the plumber.

These steps limit damage, and they are safe for most households.

Shut the main water valve.

Know where it is. A burst pipe stops when the main is off.

Stop using drains.

If a drain is backing up, adding water makes it worse.

Keep clear of wet electrics.

Do not touch outlets, panels, or appliances standing in water.

Gas smell means leave.

Leave the building and call the gas utility or 911 before you call us.
