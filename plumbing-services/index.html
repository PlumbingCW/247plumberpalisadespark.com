Drains and sewers.

A slow sink or tub often starts with drain cleaning. When snaking fails or a clog keeps returning, hydro jetting scours the pipe wall. If several drains slow together, ask about sewer line cleaning.

To see what is inside a buried pipe, a sewer camera inspection shows it. For a cracked or collapsed line, sewer line repair is the next step.

Water heaters.

A tank that leaks or a heater that gives no hot water is a water heater repair call. When the tank is too far gone, water heater replacement comes next. Planned changes fall under water heater installation.

Tankless units have their own needs, covered on the tankless water heater page. Palisades Park water is very hard, so scale is a regular topic.

Leaks and pipes.

A high water bill or a damp wall calls for leak detection. A pipe that has broken and is flooding needs burst pipe repair right away. For the supply from the street to the house, see water line repair.

Old or failing pipes throughout a house may be a case for repiping. A plumber can tell you whether repairs or a larger project make sense.

Fixtures and installation.

For a running or leaking fixture, look at toilet repair, faucet repair, or garbage disposal repair. New fixtures, appliance hookups, and remodels are handled under plumbing installation.

Shops and restaurants along Broad Avenue can ask about commercial plumbing.

Basements and water intrusion.

A damp or flooding basement calls for a sump pump check and sometimes basement waterproofing. Ridgefield, on the Hackensack River flood plain, shows why drainage matters.

Not sure where your problem fits? Describe it on the call. A dispatcher will point you to the right kind of plumber.

Before you hire anyone.

Ask for the license number and check it with the NJ Division of Consumer Affairs. Ask for a clear price before work starts, and ask who pulls the permit. Permit rules vary, so ask the Palisades Park Building Department.
