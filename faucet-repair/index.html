In Palisades Park, NJ, emergency faucet repair covers broken supply valves, failed fixture connections and handles that no longer shut off. Our 24/7 emergency dispatch covers nights and weekends.

While you wait, close the shutoff valve under the sink or at the fixture by turning it clockwise. If it will not close, shut the main water valve and open a low faucet to drain the line. Keep wet cords and outlets clear.

When a broken supply valve is an emergency.

The small shutoff valves under sinks and behind toilets are the first line of defense, and they fail more than people expect. A valve that cracks, spins loose or sprays at the stem can flood a room in minutes. Old valves also seize shut from mineral buildup, so they are not there when you need them.

If a valve is spraying and will not close, shut the main and call the 24/7 line. We offer same-day appointments when available and after-hours dispatch for active leaks. For a pipe that has split rather than a valve, see burst pipe repair.

Faucet and fixture problems we repair.

Most faucet complaints fall into a handful of causes, and each has a fix.

Steady drip from the spout.

A worn cartridge, washer or seat is the usual cause. Replacing the internal part often saves the faucet.

Leak at the handle or base.

Worn O-rings or a loose packing nut let water seep out around the handle or collar.

Low pressure or weak flow.

Mineral buildup in the aerator or cartridge slows the stream. Cleaning or replacing the part may solve it.

Handle stiff or loose.

Scale on the stem or a stripped screw often explains it.

Water under the sink.

Check supply lines, shutoff valves and the drain connection. The source decides the repair.

What to do before a plumber arrives.

These steps limit damage and keep the area safe while you wait.

  1. 1

    Close the small valve.

    Look under the sink for the two angle stops. Turn each clockwise until it stops. If they will not move, do not force them.

  2. 2

    Clear the cabinet.

    Pull out stored items and lay a towel under the supply lines. Dry cabinets are easier to inspect.

  3. 3

    Keep power away from water.

    If water reached an outlet or a disposal switch, leave it off and stay clear. Do not touch wet electrical parts.

  4. 4

    Note what you see.

    A photo of the drip point and the faucet brand label helps the dispatcher plan the parts.

Shower valves, showerheads and tub faucets.

A shower that drips after you turn it off usually has a worn cartridge inside the wall valve. Changing it means opening the trim, shutting off the supply and pulling the old cartridge. A showerhead that sprays sideways is most often clogged with mineral deposits and can be cleaned or replaced.

A tub spout that leaks when the shower is running has a failed diverter. If the water runs from the spout after you switch to the shower, that part needs attention. Water behind a wall is a different matter, so call us if you see stains.

Slow shower or tub drains.

Hair and soap build up in a tub or shower drain and slow it long before it stops. A plastic drain tool or a snake clears most of it. If the problem returns, the clog may be deeper, and our drain cleaning crew can reach the line.

Chemical drain openers can harm older pipes and are unsafe to splash. We use mechanical methods instead.

Hard water and fixtures in Palisades Park.

The Veolia Water New Jersey 2025 report lists hardness at 210 ppm as calcium carbonate, which it calls very hard. Scale can clog aerators and showerheads, make handles stiff and shorten the life of cartridges. Cleaning aerators with vinegar every few months helps. We do not diagnose health effects of water.

How we repair a leaking faucet.

A tidy repair follows the same order each time.

  1. 1

    Shut off the water.

    We close the fixture's valves first, or the main if a valve is failing.

  2. 2

    Take it apart.

    Handles, caps and retaining nuts come off, and we inspect the cartridge, seat and seals.

  3. 3

    Replace the failed parts.

    We match the manufacturer's part and clean mineral deposits from the body.

  4. 4

    Test and finish.

    We turn the water on slowly, check for leaks under pressure and make sure the handle works smoothly.

Outdoor spigots and permits.

An outdoor spigot that drips from the stem or leaks inside the wall needs prompt attention, especially before freezing weather. Palisades Park has about 7,668 housing units, many with older exterior hose bibs that deserve a look. We also serve Leonia and nearby areas.

Replacing a faucet is usually a simple fixture swap, but permit rules vary, so ask the Palisades Park Building Department, which bases plumbing fees on the number of fixtures. Work must be done by a licensed master plumber or authorized contractor.
