When a leak shows up in the ceiling.

Water follows the lowest path, so the stain you see is rarely right under the leak. In a multistory building, a pinhole in a supply line can run along a joist or down a pipe chase before it drips. That is why we trace moisture before cutting drywall, using a moisture meter and, where it helps, thermal imaging or acoustic listening gear.

Fort Lee's tower stock grew fast from the 1960s and 1970s, with buildings such as Horizon Towers (1968), The Colony (1972) and The Plaza (1975) rising along the Palisade Avenue and Boulevard East corridor. Buildings that tall have long vertical risers, so find your unit's shutoff before you need it and ask your building manager where the riser shutoff sits. A leak detection visit then opens only the damaged section.

Signs that need a call tonight.

Some warning signs can wait until morning. These cannot, because water keeps moving while you sleep.

A growing ceiling stain.

A brown ring that spreads or paint that bulges means water is still arriving.

A warm, damp wall.

A wall that feels warm may hide a leak on a hot water line.

A moving water meter.

Close every tap and check the meter. Movement with all fixtures off means a leak somewhere.

Hissing behind walls.

A hiss or running sound with everything shut off points to pressurized pipe.

A drain gurgling upstairs.

Gurgling on several floors can mean a blocked stack rather than a single clogged sink.

Water heaters and very hard water.

Veolia's 2025 water quality report lists a highest detected hardness of 210 ppm, which it classes as very hard. Hard water leaves mineral scale on heating elements and tank bottoms, so tanks rumble, run out of hot water early or leak at the base. If water is pooling under the tank, shut the cold supply to it, switch off power or gas control, and call.

A tank that has failed at the shell cannot be patched. Water heater replacement in a condo often comes down to fitting the new unit through the same closet door and along the same vent path, so tell us the tank size and where it sits.
