Until we arrive, stop using water, keep the lowest drain area clear of people and pets, and keep your distance from any wet outlet or appliance.

Sewer line cleaning clears the large pipe that carries all household waste from your building to the street. It uses a heavy cable machine, a high-pressure jetter, or both, and usually starts at the cleanout.

Find your cleanout before we arrive.

A cleanout is a capped pipe, usually 3 or 4 inches wide, that gives direct access to the main line. Common places are a basement floor near the foundation wall, a crawl space, a utility area, or outside near the house. It often has a square or round cap that can be unscrewed with a wrench.

Do not open a cleanout under pressure. If water is standing in the pipe, the cap can release a rush of waste. Just tell us where it is, and we will open it safely with the right gear. If you cannot find one, we can often work through a toilet instead.

Main sewer line clog emergency: what to do first.

A blocked main is a sanitation issue, so protecting people and property comes before cleaning.

  1. 1

    Stop all water use.

    Flushes, showers, laundry and dishwashers all send more water toward the blockage.

  2. 2

    Move belongings off the floor.

    Basement items, rugs and stored boxes near the floor drain are the first to be damaged.

  3. 3

    Stay clear of wet electrical areas.

    If a panel, outlet or furnace is near standing water, do not enter.

  4. 4

    Call the 24/7 line.

    Describe which drains are backing up. We can often arrange same-day appointments when available, and after-hours dispatch if the backup is active.

Signs the main line is the problem.

A clog in a single sink is local. These patterns point to the main.

Multiple fixtures back up together.

A toilet flush that makes the tub gurgle means water is looking for another way out.

Basement floor drain overflows first.

The lowest drain in the house is usually the first to show a main line blockage.

Gurgling from the toilet.

Air trapped by a blockage escapes through the nearest water seal.

Sewage odor.

A strong smell near the floor drain or cleanout suggests the line is full.

Repeat backups.

If the line clears and fails again within weeks, something in the pipe is feeding the problem.

How we clear a main sewer line.

We usually open the cleanout, feed a heavy cable with a cutting head, and work back and forth until water runs freely. Then we flush the line to confirm flow. For stubborn or recurring blockages we move to hydro jetting, which cleans the pipe wall with high-pressure water.

Our drain cleaning work covers fixture lines, while sewer line cleaning covers the large pipe that all those lines join. If a branch is the only problem, you do not need a main line job, and we say so.

Tree roots in the sewer line.

Roots look for moisture and enter through tiny gaps at joints. Once inside, they grow into a mat that catches paper and waste. A cable with a cutting head clears them, but roots grow back if the opening stays. If the camera shows repeated entry at one joint, a spot repair may be a better long-term answer than cleaning again.

What the camera adds after cleaning.

Clearing a clog fixes the symptom, but it does not tell you why the line blocked. A camera run after cleaning shows whether the pipe is sound, whether roots are present, and whether the line sags. Our sewer camera inspection page explains what you receive.

If the footage shows damage, the next step is sewer line repair. If the pipe looks clean, we help you understand what to avoid sending down the drain.

Wipes, grease and what not to flush.

Wipes labeled flushable do not break down like toilet paper. They collect in the main line and bind with grease and roots. Cooking fat, coffee grounds and feminine products also cause blockages. Only toilet paper and waste should go down a toilet, and fats should go in the trash.

In multi-unit buildings, one tenant's habits can block the line for everyone. Landlords and property managers should post a simple notice, and they can call the 24/7 line for after-hours backups.
