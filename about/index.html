We are a 24/7 dispatch line, not a storefront with a crew. Here is what that means, what we do, and what we do not do.

Palisades Park 24/7 Plumbing is a dispatch service for homeowners, tenants, and business owners in Palisades Park, NJ. You call the 24/7 line, describe the problem, and we connect you with a local licensed plumber who handles the work. There is no web form and no online booking, only a phone call.

This page explains how that works, how you can verify any plumber's license yourself, and where our limits are. We would rather you know them before you call.

What the service is.

We take the call, ask what is happening, and pass the details to a licensed plumber who covers your area. The plumber, not the dispatch line, does the work and holds the license. We work from 325 Broad Avenue in Palisades Park, and the line is staffed around the clock, every day of the week.

Typical calls include clogged drains, water heater trouble, burst pipes, and sewer backups. Planned jobs like fixture installation are welcome too.

Why we are call-only.

Plumbing problems are hard to describe in a form. A person on the line can ask whether water is still running, whether anyone smells gas, and whether the main valve is shut. Those questions change what you should do in the next five minutes.

A call also lets you say what you can see and hear, and lets us tell you plainly if the job is outside what we can arrange. You get a clear price before work starts from the plumber who comes to the house, and you can say no.

How to verify any plumber's New Jersey license.

You should do this for any plumber, ours or someone else. It takes a few minutes.

  1. 1

    Know who may do plumbing work.

    In New Jersey, plumbing work is limited to licensed master plumbers, authorized plumbing contractors, or their W-2 employees.

  2. 2

    Ask for the license number.

    A real contractor will give it. The number must appear on commercial vehicles, invoices, and advertising, often marked as Plumbing Lic.

  3. 3

    Check with the state.

    Look the number up with the NJ Division of Consumer Affairs, which oversees the State Board of Examiners of Master Plumbers.

  4. 4

    Match the name.

    The name on the license should match the company on the truck and the invoice.

  5. 5

    Ask about the permit.

    Permit rules vary, so ask the Palisades Park Building Department, and ask your plumber who is pulling the permit.

What we do not do.

Honest limits matter more than big claims.

Quote a price without a look.

We cannot say your exact cost before a plumber sees the job. We can explain what drives it.

Handle gas leaks.

If you smell gas, leave the building and call the gas utility or 911 first. Then call us.

Diagnose health effects of water.

We can talk plumbing. Health questions about your water go to your doctor and your water supplier.

Promise a clock time.

We offer same-day appointments when available and 24/7 emergency dispatch, but not a fixed arrival time.
