What to do in the first 10 minutes of a plumbing emergency.

A calm first ten minutes can save a ceiling, a floor or a business.

  1. 1

    Stop the water.

    Close the fixture valve, or the main shutoff if you cannot find the source. Turn clockwise.

  2. 2

    Protect yourself from electricity.

    Never stand in water near outlets, cords or the panel. If you cannot reach the breaker dry, leave it.

  3. 3

    Smell gas? Leave first.

    Leave the building and call the gas utility or 911 from outside before you call any plumber.

  4. 4

    Stop using drains.

    Do not keep flushing or running sinks if the water is backing up.

  5. 5

    Photograph and contain.

    Take photos for insurance, move belongings off wet floors, and use towels and buckets.

  6. 6

    Call the 24/7 line.

    Describe what you see. We guide you from there.

Hard water and what it does to your plumbing.

Palisades Park is on Veolia Water New Jersey's Hackensack system. The 2025 water quality report lists hardness at up to 210 ppm as calcium carbonate, which is very hard. Hard water builds scale in water heaters, faucets and shower heads, so flushing a tank and replacing worn fixtures helps. If you suspect scale is shortening a heater's life, see water heater repair.

We do not test water or make health claims. For quality questions, contact Veolia customer service.

After the July 2022 boil water advisory.

In July 2022, E. coli was found in the Veolia system, and a boil water advisory covered seven towns, including Palisades Park. It was linked to a water main break in Ridgefield and lifted on July 24. Residents were told to run faucets for three to five minutes and clean automatic ice makers.

If a similar advisory ever happens, follow the utility's instructions first. Afterward, flushing taps and cleaning ice makers are practical steps, and a plumber can check anything that seems off, such as a pressure change or discolored water.
