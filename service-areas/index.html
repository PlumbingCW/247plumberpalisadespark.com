Palisades Park, ZIP 07650.

Palisades Park is our home, a borough of about 1.25 square miles, incorporated in 1899. Broad Avenue and Bergen Boulevard hold the main business strips, and US Route 46 runs through the borough. The Building Department at Borough Hall handles plumbing permits and inspections, which run on Tuesday and Thursday mornings.

Calls here range from drain cleaning to water heater replacement and commercial plumbing for the shops and restaurants along Broad Avenue.

How the water and permits change by town.

Veolia Water New Jersey supplies the Hackensack system that serves Palisades Park, and its 2025 report lists very hard water at 210 ppm. Scale on heaters and fixtures is a common finding here, which is why water heater repair and tankless service come up often.

Each borough has its own building department and permit rules. Ask the one for your address before work starts, since permit rules vary.

Emergency service in every town.

The 24/7 line takes calls from every town on this page. Shut the main valve, stop using affected drains, and call. If you smell gas, leave first and call the gas utility or 911.

A plumber arrives as soon as one is available, but we do not give a fixed arrival time.
