Plumbing installation covers adding or relocating fixtures, supply and drain lines, gas piping and water treatment equipment. If you are remodeling a bathroom, finishing a basement or adding an appliance in Palisades Park, NJ, this is the work that puts it in. We plan the job, price it clearly, and make sure it is built for inspection.

NJ plumbing work must be done by a licensed master plumber, an authorized plumbing contractor, or their W-2 employees. You can ask for the license number, and it must appear on vehicles, invoices and advertising. You can also verify it with the NJ Division of Consumer Affairs.

New construction and remodel plumbing.

Rough-in is where plumbing begins, with supply lines, drains and vents set before walls are closed. Getting it right matters because changes later mean opening finished surfaces. We review the plans with you, locate fixtures, and check that the drain slope, vent paths and shutoffs make sense for how you will use the space.

New work in additions and gut renovations follows the same logic as small projects, just on a larger scale. We coordinate with other trades so the plumbing is in before drywall goes up.

Gas line installation and inspection: safety first.

If you smell gas, leave the building immediately and call the gas utility or 911 from outside. Do not turn on lights, use a phone inside, or look for the source. Call us only after you are safe and the utility has cleared the building. We do not promise gas leak response.

For planned work, we install and pressure-test gas piping to connect appliances. Gas work is among the most tightly regulated plumbing jobs, so permit and inspection steps matter, and we explain them before we begin.

Hard water, softeners and filtration.

Veolia's 2025 water quality report for the Hackensack system lists a highest detected hardness of 210 ppm as calcium carbonate, which is considered very hard. That means scale on fixtures, heater elements and shower heads. If you are considering a softener, test your own tap first so you know what you are treating.

We install softeners, filters and conditioners, and we repair existing units. We do not make health claims about any water treatment product. For water quality questions, contact Veolia customer service.

Water conditioner and softener repair.

A softener that stops working usually has a salt bridge, a clogged injector, a failed valve head or a drain line problem. We troubleshoot the system, restore what can be restored, and tell you honestly if replacing the unit costs less over time.

Regular salt checks and cleaning the brine tank help, and following the manufacturer's instructions for your model is the best guide.

Permits, inspections and doing it by the book.

Permit rules vary, so ask the Palisades Park Building Department. The borough's fees for plumbing permits use the number of fixtures and appliances involved, and plumbing inspections are held Tuesday and Thursday mornings. A final inspection must be made, or penalties can be issued.

We plan work with that process in mind. A job that is built to pass inspection protects you when you sell or refinance.
